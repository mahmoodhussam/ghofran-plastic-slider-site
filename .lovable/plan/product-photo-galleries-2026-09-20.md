# Product photo galleries

## What will change
- Add a representative catalog photo to every product category card.
- Make every category clickable and open a focused product gallery.
- Show multiple available products for the selected type, each with a name, image, and concise description based on the uploaded catalog.
- Add clear previous/next controls, thumbnails, and a close control.

## Experience
- Keep the existing red industrial branding and current page sections.
- Desktop galleries use a large product image beside its details.
- Mobile galleries stack the photo and details with touch-friendly controls.
- Users can close with Escape and browse with arrow keys.

## Technical details
- Keep this entirely in the existing React site.
- Create focused product crops from the uploaded catalog pages so the gallery shows products rather than full brochure spreads.
- Use the existing site controls and semantic color system.

## Verification
- Check every category card and gallery, image navigation, keyboard controls, and desktop/mobile layouts in the live preview.
